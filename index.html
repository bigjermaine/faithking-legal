Questions

Does app lock work without Screen Time?
You grant Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls permission once. Locking uses Apple’s system shields — we don’t see your passwords or messages.
Is my prayer and journal data sold?
No. Core habit data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for AI and analytics details.
Android?
Faith Kings and Queens is iPhone-only today because Apple provides the Screen Time APIs we use for app lock.
