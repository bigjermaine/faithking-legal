Questions

Do I have to use Morning pause?
No. Prayer, check-in, memory verses, and quiz work fully without it. Pause lives in Me and is off by default.
What unlocks the pause?
When pause is on, the apps you chose stay paused until today’s heart check-in (verse), today’s prayer, and a memory-verse review are finished. If you finish those before pause time, the apps stay open.
Does pause need Screen Time?
Yes, only if you turn Morning pause on. You grant Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls once. We use Apple’s system shields — we don’t see your passwords or messages.
Is my prayer and journal data sold?
No. Core habit data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for AI and analytics details.
Android?
Faith Kings and Queens is iPhone-only today. Morning pause uses Apple’s Screen Time APIs.
